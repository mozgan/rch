# R2.1 stability scope

Natural arms retain C2/C3/C4 adaptive policies. Matched-resolution arms are controlled interventions, not unmodified native rules. They freeze each rule's domain/depth across both frames and all contamination levels; all six cells share physical axis cell widths. These grid parameters are symmetric in the two frames and use the predeclared full contamination design; they are not a deployable grid estimator trained only on clean observations. Frame includes center and axes, not orientation alone. Very coarse grids, clipping, collisions and zero contrasts must be reported; predeclared depth and grid-origin sensitivities are included without changing fitted frames. Multiple rules may coincide under the intervention; this is reported, not treated as additional independent evidence. D0 baselines are recomputed under each matched quantizer. Only original inlier IDs contribute to Kendall. No frame superiority claim is justified before inspecting the actual effect estimates.
